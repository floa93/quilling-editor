Help

Getting started

  • Draw paper strips freehand, or drag a shape onto the canvas (tap/click places it in the center).
  • Text: click the canvas to add outlined letters in different fonts and sizes. They are hollow and wide enough to fill in with quilling, e.g. after printing.
  • Select elements to move, scale (corner handles) and rotate (round handle). Color and strip width apply to the selection.
  • Your design is saved in this browser automatically. Export SVG to keep a file you can import again later.
